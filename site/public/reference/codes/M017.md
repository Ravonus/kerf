# M017 (error)

`ctx.media` or a `/_weft/m/` URL used with no `media.server` provider.

**Fix:** Add `media()` from `@weft-js/plugin-media` to `plugins` in weft.config.ts.

**Status in weft@0.1.1:** reported by @weft-js/start when it serves or renders the route, and by weft check and `weft routes --meta`.
