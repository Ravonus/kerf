weft · design language · foundations

warp.

The threads everything else is woven onto. One expression dial, from a quiet professional system to a vivid creative one, sets type, corners, colour temperature, borders, depth and motion together; the sweep and the floating border label run at every stop. Every stop passes contrast in light and dark, on any pointer, with or without motion.

01Type

Seven roles, one tracking each

Display and title carry the stop's voice; heading, body, label and caption stay readable sans. Caps appear only in eyebrows and (at vivid) buttons, never below 11px; the floated field label is the one 10px exception (11px on touch). Headline emphasis is upright accent, except vivid's real serif italic: nothing is ever slanted by the browser. Figures are tabular at every stop. Sizes shown are live for this viewport; phones step body up to 16px.

02Colour & surfaces

Neutral ladder, one accent, four statuses

Temperature moves with the dial: slate at quiet, graphite at balanced, warm paper and ink at vivid. Ratios are measured in your browser from the rendered colours. The build fails anything under 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for UI and focus.

03Shape & depth

Hairlines, corners, elevation, focus

Containers use one 1px hairline. The emphasis line (selection, tabs, sweep) thickens from 1 to 2px across the dial. Quiet floats on soft shadows; vivid sits flat and lets borders do the work. The focus ring is the same 2px everywhere.

04Base controls

The signature: sweep and floating border

Two stacked 1px borders. On hover or focus the accent border draws in left to right while the resting one wipes away; on leave it keeps travelling right instead of rewinding. The label lifts into a notch cut from whatever is behind it, and its letters tint one by one as the stroke passes. Present at every stop; the dial only changes speed, colour and how far it reaches. Fields and checkboxes come from warp.css; buttons and cards are sketches.

05Motion

Things arrive, settle and leave faster

Enters ease out and stagger; exits are 70% as long, half the distance, accelerate away and never stagger. The sweep runs at every stop (480, 620, 700ms on (0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)). Under reduced motion it becomes an instant border-colour change and the label a 120ms fade, and nothing loops: shimmers and dashes stop, a spinner becomes a slow opacity pulse.

06Form factors

One card UI on phone, tablet and desktop

Each frame is a real 390, 834 or 1280px wide container, scaled to fit. Layout answers container queries; the frame sets data-form and data-pointer so type steps and 44px touch targets switch exactly as the media queries would on a device.

Why containers, not iframeswarp components respond to their container, not the window, so a width-constrained container is the honest test. An iframe would also re-run media queries, which the frame reproduces with two attributes. One document keeps every frame in sync with the toolbar.
TouchOn a coarse pointer, md and lg controls grow to 44 and 48px, sm keeps its visual size with a 44px hit area, and input text never drops below 16px, so iOS doesn't zoom.
LayoutUnder 600px: one column and a bottom action dock. 600–999px: two columns, actions move to the header. 1000px and up: the sidebar appears and Members spans two rows.
