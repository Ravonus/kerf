weft · warp · inputs

Fields.

One field shell carries every input, from a text box to a colour picker: a border that sweeps left to right under the pointer, a label that rises into a notch in that border, and a message line below. Flip Fields in the bar to Classic and every field on this page puts its label above the box instead; the sweep stays. Errors wait until you finish, then clear as you fix them.

01The shell

One shell, two borders, a notch

Every field is a control holding two stacked borders: the resting sweep-base (ink) and the accent sweep. On hover or focus the sweep draws in from the left while the base wipes away under it; when you leave it keeps travelling right and out. The label sits inside at rest and rises into a notch on hover, focus or value; each character turns accent as the sweep passes it. Slots (prefix, suffix, clear, spinner, stepper, chevron) live inside the one box. The diagram is frozen mid-sweep.

02Validation

Late to complain, quick to forgive

No error appears while you are typing a value for the first time. A field is checked when you leave it or submit; once it has shown an error it re-checks on every key, so the danger sweep lets go the moment the value is fixed. Errors pin the sweep in critical and the message slides in under the field. Async checks show a spinner in the end slot.

      08More

      Beyond the original

      The pickers and form mechanics other kits ship that the old toolkit lacked. Each one is still the shell: a swept host, the label that floats (or sits above in Classic), the message line. Knobs and the XY pad are swept boxes with a rail inside; the cascader and tree select are the select trigger with a wider popup.

        09Motion

        Sweep, float, fail · at every stop

        The sweep is present at every stop; the dial sets its colour and speed. Sweep pins every control in a cell so you can compare them; Fail pins the danger sweep and, at vivid only, shakes once. Under reduced motion the sweep becomes an instant colour change and the label fades instead of travelling; the end states are identical.
