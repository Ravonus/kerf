02Table

A real <table>, with every grid feature

Sort (aria-sort on the header), filter with the floating-border field, column visibility, resize (a window-splitter separator: drag or ←/→), pinned first columns, row selection with shift-range and a bulk bar, expandable rows, inline edit (Enter or double-click a budget), density, and the four states. Rows are keyed: sorting and paging move nodes, they never rebuild them. Narrow the window, or look at the frames below.

05Charts

Hand-written SVG, one interaction model

Tab into a chart, then use the arrow keys: ←/→ walk the x axis, ↑/↓ change series, Home/End jump. Hover or tap-and-drag shows the same tooltip. Every chart has a table toggle and a summary sentence. Switch the dial: quiet is flat hairlines and no motion, balanced fades in, vivid draws in with washes and serif notes.

          07Kanban

          Drag on a pointer, move from a menu

          Columns are lists. With a mouse, drag a card. On touch, or anywhere, use the card's move button. With a keyboard, focus a card and press Alt + ←/→ to change column or Alt + ↑/↓ to reorder; every move is announced. Cards are moved, never re-created, so their state and focus survive.

          08Grid at scale

          When the table becomes a grid

          Editing, ranges and clipboard turn a table into role="grid": one tab stop, arrows move the cell, Shift + arrows extend a range, ⌘/Ctrl + C copies it as TSV, Enter edits (with validation), Escape cancels. Drag a header (or Alt + ←/→ on it) to reorder. Saved views store columns, filters, sort and grouping by name. Groups carry their own subtotal; the footer aggregates what is visible.
