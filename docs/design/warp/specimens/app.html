warp · app patterns · v0.3

The parts of an app that move on their own.

Calendars people drag, rooms people talk in, inboxes that fill while you watch, threads, and cursors that aren't yours. Agents are the flagship: they stream, reason, call tools and ask before they act. One grammar for anything that arrives: it enters from above, the sweep draws its edge once, and a live region says it out loud.

01 · AI & agent UIs · flagship

Show the work. Ask before acting.

The reasoning block streams, then folds to one line with its duration. Tokens stream into real markdown behind a 2px accent caret with no layout jump. Tool calls run and settle as cards; a write waits on an approval you can edit. Citations are numbered, every answer has actions and branches, and the plan on the right is a live task queue.

  • Pick a starter prompt or press Send; Stop keeps what arrived.
  • Type / for commands; open the model chip; watch the context meter.
  • Approve, edit or deny the write step. Regenerate, then flip branches with ‹ 2/2 ›.
02 · Calendar & scheduling

Drag it on a desk. Hold it on a phone.

Month, week, day, agenda and timeline share one event model. Pointer users drag to create, move and resize on a 15-minute snap. Touch users get an agenda first, swipe between days, and long-press for a move sheet. Keyboard users get both, without a mouse.

  • Drag across an empty slot to create; drag an event to move; pull its bottom edge to resize.
  • Tab to an event, then Enter for details, M to move with arrows, Shift+↓ to resize, Enter to drop.
  • On the phone: swipe the day, or hold an event to move it.
  • Open Year: arrows walk the days, PgDn a month, M opens the month, Enter the day.
03 · Notifications & connection

Things arrive. Nothing shouts.

The inbox is the record; toasts are a courtesy for what you'd otherwise miss. New items enter from above with one sweep across their top edge, the badge bumps once, and a polite live region reads a summary. The .live status stays small until the connection is in trouble.

  • Press New arrival or Burst and watch the inbox, badge and toasts.
  • Switch the connection: Reconnecting and Offline surface a banner; returning to Live replays what queued as one summary.
  • Close the inbox: arrivals become toasts instead.
  • Below: arrow through the preferences grid, flip a group toggle, pick a digest. In the transport picker, block WebSockets or go lossy.

Connection status, four states

One component, read from query.live().live, stream().status or channel.status. Live is quiet; trouble is coloured and gets words.

04 · Messaging

A thread that tells you where every message is.

Sending, sent, read, failed with retry. Grouped by author, a hairline marks what's new, reactions are toggle buttons, and the composer is the signature field: its border sweeps when you focus it, and it never hides behind the keyboard.

  • Type @ in the composer for mentions; Enter sends, Shift+Enter breaks a line.
  • Retry the failed message. Toggle a reaction.
  • Hover a message (or focus it and press Q) to quote it; select a few words first to quote only those. Jump to original finds the source.
  • On the phone, open the keyboard: the composer rides on top of it.
05 · Video meetings

The room gets out of the way of the people.

Prejoin checks your devices, the lobby waits honestly, and the room has four layouts. The active speaker's tile edge is drawn by the sweep: in when they start, out to the right when they stop. Muted, hand raised and poor network are flags, never colour alone.

  • Switch layouts; toggle mic, camera, hand, share, chat and people.
  • Drag the self-view; it snaps to the nearest corner.
  • Tab into the control bar; each control says its state.
06 · Collaboration, comments & sharing

Other people, without the noise.

Presence avatars carry a colour ring that matches their cursor, selection and edit lock. Cursors glide on remote updates and fade when idle. A field someone holds says so and who; a conflict shows both versions side by side. Comments anchor to a text range and live in a margin rail; Share sets a role per person.

  • Watch two cursors on the brief; press Follow on an avatar to ride their viewport.
  • Reply to or resolve a comment. Open Share.
  • Try the locked field. Resolve the conflict.
  • In the 403, request access, then press Owner approves; or switch account.
Motion · per dial stop

Good, not flashy.

The current stop is highlighted. Reduced motion keeps every state and drops travel: arrivals fade, the sweep becomes a colour change, the now line appears without drawing, and streaming text stops fading in per token.
