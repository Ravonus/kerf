(01) Motion presets

Six fancy entrances, one governor

CRT power-on and off, flicker, glitch, spotlight and iris. Each is a single run of 400ms or less on transform, opacity and clip-path, and only at vivid. Every other stop, and reduced motion, gets that stop's plain enter or exit.

Pick a preset

Spotlight opens from where you clicked the stage (from the centre for a keyboard press). Flicker dips twice in 400ms and ignores a repeat within a second, so it never passes two flashes a second.

(02) MotionCard

Cards that answer a press

A selectable card. Balanced lifts the shadow on hover and presses to 0.98. Vivid adds a 2px rise and 1.01 scale on hover, the one place a card moves on hover. Quiet and reduced motion stay flat.

(03) Media

Custom controls over a native video. Captions, chapters on the timeline, keyboard control and a generated poster. The chapters are timeline() cues, the same boxes a weft app binds to.

      Words that catch the light

            1. 22 Oct 2026v2.4

              Canary budgets

              Give each rollout a cost and eval budget. Tensile pauses the canary the moment either is spent, and keeps the trace that spent it.
